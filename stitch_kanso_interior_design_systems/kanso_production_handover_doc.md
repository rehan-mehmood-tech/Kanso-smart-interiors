# Kanso Design System & Component Library (Production Handover)

## 1. Brand Identity: "Quiet Luxury"
The Kanso brand is built on the principles of architectural minimalism, editorial elegance, and "calm tech." The visual language prioritizes the "hero" (the interior design) through intentional whitespace, a muted but warm color palette, and high-contrast, sophisticated typography.

### Design Tokens
- **Surface**: `#fbf9f4` (Warm Alabaster)
- **Primary/Text**: `#171717` (Charcoal Black)
- **Accent/Secondary**: `#dcdad5` (Stone Grey)
- **Typography**: Noto Serif (Display & Headlines), Sans-serif (Body & Labels)
- **Roundness**: 8px (Soft Architectural)

## 2. Core Components Library

### TopNavBar
A minimalist, high-contrast navigation bar used on marketing and introductory screens.
- **Background**: `bg-surface`
- **Typography**: `font-body-md`
- **CTA**: "Design My Room" (Primary Action)

### SideNavBar (Kanso Pro)
The functional navigation for the specialist workspace, designed for density and focus.
- **Background**: `bg-surface-container`
- **Tabs**: Dashboard, Projects, Analytics, Clients, Settings.

### Lead & Project Cards
Consistent card structures across both consumer and pro experiences.
- **Style**: Subtle borders (`border-outline-variant`), soft shadows, and generous internal padding.

## 3. Key UX Flows Documented
- **New Project Wizard**: 4-step linear flow with high-fidelity visual feedback.
- **AI Generation State**: Calm, non-technical loading experience.
- **Lead Matching & Specialist Onboarding**: Conversion-focused journey from inspiration to execution.
